# Problem statement

Outpatient histories are fragmented across printed reports, images and clinic-specific documents. Patients may not find an earlier result during a consultation. ShebaMed organizes patient-held records without claiming access to external hospital systems.

The MVP addresses retrieval and context, not diagnosis or clinical decision automation. Success should be evaluated by source retrieval accuracy, extraction correctness, scope enforcement and time to locate a known synthetic report. No unverified population statistics are used.
